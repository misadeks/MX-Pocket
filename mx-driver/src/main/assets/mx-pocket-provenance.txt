Cross-check: https://github.com/mario-gutierrez/mx-creative-console-webhid
Revision: 6a70b6db14d0b6c0a867f873aa0a4b571026d70a
MIT License; Copyright (c) 2026 Mario Gutierrez.
Sources: js/feature19a1.js, js/feature1b04.js, js/mxCreativeConsole.js.
Confirms JPEG report payload and HID++ 1b04 lookup. Its pragmatic displayIndex=0,
24-bit length, masked sequence and unacknowledged sends differ from the primary
source; MX Pocket follows the primary's displayIndex=1, 16-bit image limit,
unmasked part index and per-fragment acknowledgements instead.

Experimental reference (not copied into runtime):
https://github.com/hcooper-idealbuilders/mx-creative-controller/blob/main/docs/protocol.md
Revision: 221c52f3514a25671a08fa19e723e49c44119543
Ownership/keepalive guesses, captured startup packets and presumed collection
to-interface mappings are not verified facts and are not replayed by this app.

Android API: https://developer.android.com/develop/connectivity/usb/host
Hardware: https://support.logi.com/hc/en-ch/articles/24727157859351-Specification-MX-Creative-Console
Official VID/PID: 046D:C354; 9 LCD buttons, 2 additional buttons; 480x480
panel specification. The primary driver treats the panel as one 480x480 surface,
with nine 118x118 visible regions at x=23+158*column, y=6+158*row.
This layout is source-supported, not measured on attached hardware.

No Android phone or keypad was accessible during the initial build. Unit tests
and Android API simulation are not physical USB/LCD verification. USB descriptors,
kernel claim success, actual display visibility/persistence, all 11 controls,
reconnect and phone power compatibility must be confirmed on hardware.
